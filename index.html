Rock3D

AI-driven UAV Photogrammetry and 3D Rock Measurement

Master Research Project
China University of Mining and Technology (Beijing)

Research Overview

Rock3D investigates UAV-based rock recognition, multi-view photogrammetry, and 3D geometric measurement. The project aims to establish a reproducible workflow from field imagery to individual rock-scale measurement.

Research Pipeline

UAV Images ↓ AI-based Rock Recognition ↓ Image-to-3D Surface Association ↓ Multi-view 3D Geometry Completion ↓ Rock Dimension Measurement

Key Contributions

  • AI-based rock instance recognition from UAV imagery
  • Multi-view photogrammetric surface association and completion
  • 3D geometric measurement validation workflow
  • Reproducible engineering pipeline for rock analysis
