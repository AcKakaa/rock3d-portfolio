Rock3D

AI-driven UAV Photogrammetry and 3D Rock Measurement

Research Overview

Rock3D is a research project focusing on UAV-based rock recognition, multi-view photogrammetry, and 3D geometric measurement.

Research Pipeline

UAV imagery → AI-based rock recognition → 3D surface association → multi-view geometry completion → rock dimension measurement

Current Demonstration

Multi-view 3D reconstruction and measurement validation examples will be added.
