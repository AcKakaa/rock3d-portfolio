Rock3D

AI-driven UAV Photogrammetry and 3D Rock Measurement

Master Research Project
China University of Mining and Technology (Beijing)

Research Overview

Rock3D focuses on UAV-based rock recognition, multi-view photogrammetry, and 3D geometric measurement. The project develops a reproducible workflow from field imagery to individual rock-scale measurement.

Key Contributions

  • AI-based rock instance recognition from UAV imagery
  • Multi-view photogrammetric surface association and validation
  • 3D geometric measurement workflow for individual rocks
  • Reproducible engineering pipeline for rock analysis
